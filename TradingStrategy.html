Paper Bot Strategy Guide

Technical documentation for the dual-strategy automated paper trading system.

INTRADAY (5-MIN BARS)

1. Momentum & Mean Reversion

Focuses on Opening Range Breakouts (ORB) and VWAP Reclaims.

Hard Filters (Mandatory)

  • Time: Signal must fire between 10:00 AM and 2:00 PM ET.
  • OR Quality: 30-min Opening Range must be 0.3% – 3% of the stock price.
  • RSI: No entries if RSI > 80 (Long) or < 20 (Short).

SWING (DAILY BARS)

2. Trend Regime Following

Detects high-probability trend continuation patterns using daily bar closes.

Regime Requirements

  • Trend Direction: Price must be above EMA 200.
  • Alignment: EMA 20 must be above EMA 50.
  • Strength: ADX must be ≥ 20 (trending market).
  • Momentum: MACD Line > Signal Line.

Scoring (Passing: 70 pts)

  • Base Regime: 40 pts awarded if all Hard Filters pass.
  • Volume Ratio: 20 pts if volume is ≥ 1.2x average.
  • Candlestick Bonus: Bullish Engulfing (35), Morning Star (28), Hammer (22), Piercing (16).
